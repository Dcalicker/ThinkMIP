Meet the Founders

Mobility Intelligence Partners was founded by Donovan Calicker and Marcos Delgado, two transportation professionals with decades of combined leadership experience across public transit operations, safety, compliance, service delivery, and organizational strategy.

Strategy & Technology

Donovan Calicker

Chief Executive Officer

Donovan Calicker is an accomplished transportation leader with extensive experience in transportation and logistics, data analytics, contract management, operational strategy, ADA compliance and technology deployment. His ability to build high-performing teams and develop innovative operational solutions helps organizations improve performance while delivering exceptional service.

Tactical Operations

Marcos Delgado

Chief Operating Officer

Marcos Delgado brings more than 20 years of leadership experience in public transportation, specializing in transit operations, service delivery, control center and dispatch management, regulatory compliance, emergency management, and tactical operations. His practical, results-driven approach helps agencies strengthen operations, improve efficiency, and achieve measurable results.

Together, Donovan and Marcos founded Mobility Intelligence Partners with a shared vision of providing innovative, data-driven consulting solutions that help transit agencies and transportation organizations enhance safety, optimize operations, strengthen compliance, and deliver reliable, customer-focused transportation services.
